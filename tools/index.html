Unplaced

Re-rolls once per swell, at the darkest point of the cycle — so with a deep square pulse the shape lands somewhere new on every flash rather than being smeared where it stands. Still has no settled home: reach for it to lift a chorus and it is Travel, reach for it to change how a section feels and it belongs on the character surface. Colour decides what colour that pixel is

What you place

A slide runs one way across its ruler with the base colour at the centre, so the amount is how far one end departs and the two ends land twice that apart. A region is a bump — base, departure, back to base. The ruler decides what the position is measured against: across the five strips, along a strip, or from a shape's leading tip through to the end of its tail. Count, width and edge mean here exactly what they mean in the shape branch.

What lives

Colour never quite the same in two places, with the difference always moving. Two terms at the golden ratio, so it cannot come back into step and the wall never repeats — that is built in rather than dialled. Aim it nowhere; that is what the placed field is for.

From the light level

Colour read off how lit the shape branch left a pixel — the one source that reaches the pulse and jitter, since neither has any position for a ruler to measure. Reads the shape's profile only, never the colour layer's own darkness: feed that back and pulling the wall down for a quiet verse would slide its hue.

Colour looks

These set the colour layer only and leave the shape alone, so each one lands on whatever pattern is up. Head and tail and Hot core need a shape with gaps to show anything — try them on Bars or Comet.
the two multiply, once, per pixel

5 strips

fed by shape × colour — 45 pixels each
The only output that can render the shape branch at all. Everything above reaches these.

4 PARs

fed by colour + the pulse — one pixel each
A PAR is one colour, so the shape branch cannot reach it: count, width, edge, tail, speed and fan all describe positions along a strip that a single lamp has no way to show. The colour branch is exactly what a one-pixel fixture can render, which is why these two controls are a relationship to the strips rather than a second look.

Not built

How much of the pulse reaches the PARs — designed in DESIGN.md, no CC and no firmware. Until it exists the pulse either reaches them through the strips' own brightness or not at all.
