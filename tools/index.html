The beat

BPM
Both branches read this. Every speed and every rate below is per beat, so changing tempo changes rate and never jumps. Browser timing drifts a little — fine for judging motion, use a DAW if the tempo itself is what you are testing. With no clock the brain free-runs at 120.
Starting the clock also sends a transport start, which puts the brain's musical position and the preview's both back to zero — so the bar the pulse lands on is the same bar in both. Travel is not reset by it, on the wall or here.

Color decides what color that pixel is

What you place

A gradient runs one way across its ruler with the base color at the center, so the amount is how far one end departs and the two ends land twice that apart. A region is a bump — base, departure, back to base. The ruler decides what the position is measured against: across the five strips, along a strip, or from a shape's leading tip through to the end of its tail. Count, width and edge mean here exactly what they mean in the shape branch.

What lives

Color never quite the same in two places, with the difference always moving. Two terms at the golden ratio, so it cannot come back into step and the wall never repeats — that is built in rather than dialed. Aim it nowhere; that is what the placed field is for.

From the light level

Color read off how lit the shape branch left a pixel — the one source that reaches the pulse and jitter, since neither has any position for a ruler to measure. Reads the shape's profile only, never the color layer's own darkness: feed that back and pulling the wall down for a quiet verse would slide its hue.

Color looks

These set the color layer only and leave the shape alone, so each one lands on whatever pattern is up. Head and tail and Hot core need a shape with gaps to show anything — try them on Bars or Comet.
Pulse one swell, pushing on both branches and on the washes

Source

One oscillator with one rate. A destination says how far the pulse pushes it and what wave does the pushing — never how fast, because every rate here feeds a running total, so a pulse aimed at one would move the wall permanently instead of returning it.
The rate steps rather than sweeps, and the phase is anchored to the grid, so a swell lands on the downbeat instead of wherever the fader was last touched. Between the steps there is no period a bar can hold a whole number of, which is what "in time but not on time" was.

Destinations

Every destination is always connected and its amount may be zero. Nothing here is a patch cable: a morph can interpolate an amount and cannot snap a connection on, and an amount rising from zero is a connection fading in.
the two multiply, once, per pixel

5 strips

fed by shape × color — 45 pixels each
The only output that can render the shape branch at all. Everything above reaches these.

4 PARs

fed by color + the pulse — one pixel each
A PAR is one color, so the shape branch cannot reach it: count, width, edge, tail, speed and fan all describe positions along a strip that a single lamp has no way to show. The color branch is exactly what a one-pixel fixture can render, which is why these two controls are a relationship to the strips rather than a second look.

Not built

A saturation of their own, and a color sampled at each PAR's position so the four differ from each other rather than being four copies of one hue. Both designed in DESIGN.md, neither has a CC. The pulse reaches all four together: they take its unfanned phase, because a PAR is one position with no strip to be offset from.
